The 22nd Goodwill Ambassador Program _Macau

Under One Sky, Goodwill Sets Sail

Entering its 22nd edition, organized by the Rotaract Club of Macau under the guidance of the Rotary Club of Macau for youth aged 18 and above. Under the theme "Under One Sky, Goodwill Sets Sail," it leads Macao youth into Mongolia—where vast steppes meet rich history—for a goodwill journey combining deep cultural exploration, project execution, and youth friendship.

About the Programme

跨文化交流，讓親善走得更遠

以澳門文化為起點，在蒙古建立深度理解、友誼連結與青年行動。

「澳門親善大使文化交流之旅」多年來致力為本澳青年搭建走向世界、與國際接軌的橋樑。透過系統化的分組協作、方案實踐與跨文化交流，讓青年代表化身文化使者，在向外推廣澳門中西共融特色與多元文化的同時，亦深入走進當地社群，體驗不同地域的自然風貌、歷史底蘊與人文關懷。

For years, the "Macao Goodwill Ambassador Cultural Exchange Programme" has been dedicated to building a bridge connecting Macao youth with the wider world and international communities. Through structured group collaboration, project execution, and cross-cultural exchange, youth representatives step into the role of cultural ambassadors—sharing Macao's Sino-Portuguese heritage and multicultural character abroad while engaging deeply with local communities to experience diverse natural landscapes, historical depth, and human warmth.
